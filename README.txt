Lingyi China Travel website
Popular Routes section removed.

Open index.html to preview locally, or upload index.html, i18n.js,
enhancements.js, extras.js, places.js and the img directory together to your web host.

Language options: Indonesian (default for new visitors), English, Chinese.
The choice is remembered in the browser.

Contact forms: the short forms prepare a WhatsApp message; visitors must
send it in WhatsApp. The long form prepares an email that visitors must
send from their email app. No inquiry is stored on this static site.

The destination images are illustrative, including generated scenes.
Visa and entry information changes; verify current details through the
relevant official source before advising a traveler.

Destination finder: search matches city or landmark names in the selected
language and English. Sort by suggested popular order or by localized city
name. The popular order is an editorial first-trip recommendation, not
measured booking volume.

Visitors can tick places in different city guides to build a shortlist.
The selected places stay available in the current browser tab while they
browse and are included in the review panel and existing request forms.
The review panel prepares WhatsApp or email with selected places and trip
details. Visitors must press Send in WhatsApp or their email application;
the static website itself does not transmit inquiries or store personal data.

Additional destinations: 102 destinations transcribed from the supplied
screenshots appear in the same Places to explore photo grid. Each opens a
detail view with two suggested attractions, a short description of each,
illustrative images, and a checkbox for each attraction. Visitors can also
select the whole city. The grid initially shows 12 destinations. More destinations adds the next
12 each time; search and filters cover the full set. Search includes city,
province, and attraction names in English and Chinese. All selections are included in WhatsApp/email drafts.

The added city and attraction photos are generated illustrations rather than
verified photographs of exact locations. Suggested attraction access and
suitability should be confirmed while planning each custom itinerary.
